# Official local SEO and AEO guidance — Central Texas

Research date: 8 September 2026  
Scope: an Inna nutrition/wellness practice seeking discovery in Buda, Kyle, Austin, San Marcos, and genuinely served nearby Central Texas communities.

## Operating principle

The plan should establish **eligibility and relevance**, not promise rankings, Map Pack placement, rich results, or AI citations. Google says its generative Search features draw on its core Search ranking and quality systems; a page must be indexed and eligible for a normal Search snippet to be eligible, while appearance is not guaranteed. [Google’s AI-search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) therefore supports sound SEO, accurate entities, useful evidence, and clear content—not a separate “AEO hack.”

## Location-targeting architecture to approve before publishing

1. **Central Texas service-area hub:** one durable page describing the actual offer, provider, eligibility/scope, appointment method, proof of service coverage, a compact service-area list, and links to the underlying services. This is the mandatory first location page.
2. **Core service pages:** pages for distinct, genuine offerings; each links back to the hub and explains where/how clients in the four named cities can access that service.
3. **Selective city pages:** not a default set of four. Approve each only with a documented, user-meaningful differentiator. They must not simply replace “Buda” with “Kyle,” “Austin,” or “San Marcos.”
4. **Local-entity consistency:** align business name, practitioner name, direct telephone number, URL, services, hours, address/service-area treatment, and official social links across the site, GBP, Bing Places, schema, directories, and citations.
5. **Measurement gate:** record a baseline before release, then review Search Console and Bing Webmaster performance by query/page and genuine conversion data at 30/60/90 days. Monitor exact city-and-service searches from a Texas-relevant locale; do not interpret an isolated AI answer as a ranking result.

## Preconditions requiring owner confirmation

- Public professional name and legal/brand name; the screenshot shows a conflict between Inna Topiler and Inna Benyukhis.
- Direct business phone; the screenshot indicates the visible number and `tel:` destination differ.
- Actual service model (online, at a permanent client-facing location, at client locations, or hybrid), staffed address if any, hours, and the precise communities genuinely served.
- Credentials, permitted scope of advice, and sources/methodology for all outcome, health, and testimonial claims.
- Official social/profile URLs and any real-world reviews that may be displayed with consent.

These facts should be approved before any structured-data or location-page implementation, so the site does not repeat false entity information or accidentally claim a location it does not operate.
